Expected crowds this week

Each cell is the day's peak crowd index (100 = at comfortable capacity). Outlined = quietest resort that day. Tap one for the hour-by-hour estimate. Near-term days use the weather forecast when it's available; later days use measured snowpack and history.

How to read it. Crowd index: 100 means the resort is at comfortable capacity. "Crowd now" is the model's estimate for this hour, adjusted to live canyon traffic where UDOT counts it (Alta, Snowbird, Brighton, Solitude). Cars at a resort are estimated by splitting the canyon's measured total between the resorts that are open. Lift-line counts come from public webcams and only count lifts that are running.
Sources: UDOT live canyon volumes, resort lift-status pages, Ski Utah webcams and snow report, USDA SNOTEL. Estimates, not official counts.
