Opening Day is November 27th!

Join us in kicking off our 86th season. Lifts will start spinning at 9am. Learn More >

After receiving 29" of snow in the last 7 days, we're officially opening this Friday, November 27.

Whether you'll be camping out for first chair and snagging your commemorative t-shirt for being one of the first 100 visitors, or sneaking in some late-morning turns, we're excited to welcome you to the slopes for our 86th season. Join us for Opening Day on Friday, November 27! Lifts will start spinning at 9am.

Guests wanting to camp out the night before Opening Day may arrive starting at 6PM on Thursday and may only setup outside of the Needles Gondola maze. All belongings must be cleaned up by 7am on Friday morning. 

Due to snowmaking operations and our teams gearing up for Opening Day, all uphill hiking & biking access is closed. Canyon Rim Biking Trail is also closed for the season.
