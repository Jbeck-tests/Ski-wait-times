Opening Day is November 27th!

Join us in kicking off our 86th season. Lifts will start spinning at 9am. Learn More >

Happy Opening Day!! We can't wait to see all of your smiling faces back on the slopes this weekend for our 86th Winter Season! 

Remember, it's early season conditions, extra reason to ski with caution! Take it slow and enjoy the ride. Let's make this season a long one

Opening Day Terrain: Lifts will start spinning at 9am.
We're excited to offer top to bottom skiing with a mix of groomed and natural terrain.

Needles Gondola, Littlecat Express, Becker Lift and Middle Bowl Express will be open with access to:

Groomed Runs: Sweet Revenge, Dogleg, Needles Way, Porcupine Traverse, Middle bowl Traverse, Bullwinkle, Bear Hollow, Snow Shoe, School Hill, Littlecat, and Slo Road

Natural Terrain: Pork Barrel, Fro Zone, Lower Fro Zone, Dan's Run, RockyJ, Two Bit Street, Boardwalk, Upper 119, 119 and Herberts

Due to snowmaking operations and our teams gearing up for Opening Day, all uphill hiking & biking access is closed. Canyon Rim Biking Trail is also closed for the season.
