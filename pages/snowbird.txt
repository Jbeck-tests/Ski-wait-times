November 26, 2026 - 05:42 am

Happy Thanksgiving! 

Resort Update:

The Aerial Tram, Little Cloud, Gadzoom, Wilbere and Chickadee are scheduled to start spinning at 9 a.m., conditions permitting.

All uphill travel is closed and early-season conditions exist. Please be aware of objects at or below the snow surface. 

Mountain Report:

Whether you're sitting down to Thanksgiving dinner or joining us on the slopes, there are four magic words today... GIVE ME THE BIRD!

On this Turkey Day, boy oh boy am I grateful we're out skiing again! Opening Day was phenomenal and the mountain is skiing great and, spoiler alert, more of the same is in store for today. Expect a mix of sunshine and clouds with upper-mountain temps in the 30s. It could be a little windy at the top with gusts near 30mph, so it might be a good day for a buff or extra layer before you've built up your mid-winter cold tolerance. 

Our Tramsgiving event is from 12 to 3:45p, bring 3 non-perishable canned goods in exchange for one free scenic ride up the tram! All proceeds will benefit the Utah Food Bank. Great cause. Great views. What could be better? Snowbird Tramsgiving 

It’s early season, so take it easy out there. Ski and ride in control, respect closures and rope lines, and remember that lift and terrain availability can change throughout the day.

Give me The Bird.
