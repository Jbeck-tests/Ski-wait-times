November 27, 2026 - 05:45 am

Resort Update:

The Aerial Tram, Little Cloud, Gadzoom, Wilbere and Chickadee are scheduled to start spinning at 9 a.m., conditions permitting.

All uphill travel is closed and early-season conditions exist. Please be aware of objects at or below the snow surface. 

Mountain Report:

Happy Friday, folks! It's gonna be another great day at The Bird with early-season top-to-bottom skiing and the groomers riding in mid-season form. After a brief dryspell this week, the weather is starting to turn! Expect a windy day out there today ahead of a small storm overnight; we're talking 1-4 inches. Tonight is just the appetizer ahead of the main event early/mid next week when the forecasts are calling for a significant snow event. So enjoy the groomers today, the soft stuff tomorrow, and get those powder skis tuned up for next week! 

And for all you aspiring rockstars out there, we have Karaoke in the Tram Club from 3-6p today! You can shred on the mountain, but can you rip on the mic? Swing by to enjoy a drink or two, some tasty food, and performances from Little Cottonwood's greatest unknown stars. Tram Club Karaoke - 21+

It’s early season, so take it easy out there. Ski and ride in control, respect closures and rope lines, and remember that lift and terrain availability can change throughout the day.
