November 25, 2026 - 05:41 am

Opening Day is here. Welcome back to The Bird!

Resort Update:

The Aerial Tram, Little Cloud, Gadzoom, Wilbere and Chickadee are scheduled to start spinning at 9 a.m., conditions permitting.

Mountain Report:
No new snow overnight, but it's going to be a great day to get back on the hill, with temperatures on the mountain hovering in the high 30s to low 40s, light winds and some high clouds in the forecast.

I’ve heard from patrol that the mountain is skiing phenomenally well right now, and after waiting for Opening Day all summer, I can’t wait to check it out for myself.

It’s still early season, so take it easy out there. Ski and ride in control, respect closures and rope lines, and remember that lift and terrain availability can change throughout the day.

Tomorrow for our Tramsgiving event from 12:00p to 3:45p, bring 3 non-perishable canned goods in exchange for one free scenic ride up the tram! All proceeds will benefit the Utah Food Bank. Great cause. Great views. What could be better? Snowbird Tramsgiving 

That’s it from me. Have an awesome Opening Day, and I’ll see you in the lift line.

Give me The Bird.
