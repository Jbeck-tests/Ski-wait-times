November 28, 2026 - 05:45 am

Resort Update:

The Aerial Tram, Little Cloud, Gadzoom, Wilbere and Chickadee are scheduled to start spinning at 9 a.m., conditions permitting.

All uphill travel is closed and early-season conditions exist. Please be aware of objects at or below the snow surface. 

Mountain Report:

Happy Saturday! The storm that was forecasted for overnight got caught up in those classic Thanksgiving travel delays and is just now arriving at the mountain. We're now looking at 2-4 inches of fresh snow falling from 6-9a and light flurries continuing through 11a. Strong winds are accompanying the storm, so bundle up and keep an eye out for shifts to lift and terrain status throughout the day. 

But what about the skiing? How's the skiing?!? It's superb. The bumps are skiing soft and the groomers are holding an edge admirably. I'll be lapping Little Cloud today but you really can't go wrong wherever you choose to make your turns. 

No events on the calendar today, but might I suggest stopping by The Nest for their incredible Butter Chicken? It's so good that I, personally, consider it a glorious event every time I dig it. 

It’s early season, so take it easy out there. Ski and ride in control, respect closures and rope lines, and remember that lift and terrain availability can change throughout the day.
