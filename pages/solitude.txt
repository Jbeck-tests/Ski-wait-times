DAILY MESSAGE
November 27, 2026 at 5:25 a.m. — Thanksgiving is over but the holiday weekend has just begun. Burn off those extra T-Day calories the fun way — skiing and riding Solitude! Moonbeam Express, Apex Express, Link, and The Magic Carpet are open, serving 8 trails. Our mountain ops team continues to open new terrain with Eagle next in line. Stay tuned to Solitude social media for updates. The Upper Tude Dude’s Terrain Park is now open with multiple features. Variable, early-season conditions exist, including firm sections. Apex Express terrain should be considered “experts only.” Please check your speed and keep your eyes open for other skiers and riders. Solitude’s Black Friday sale is in full effect with great deals on lift tickets and lodging. Purchase an S-Card 4-pack and ski for under $80/day. Or book lodging at 30% off the regular rate. The skies will be cloudy today — and snow is expected tomorrow!
